# Policy Traceability

Maps every implemented leave and attendance rule to its policy source, the code that implements it, the automated test that verifies it, and any interpretation that needs HR sign-off.

> **Important — source wording.** The two policy PDFs (`Leave Policy R2_HO.pdf`, `Attendance Policy_R2.pdf`) and the reference screenshots were **not attached to the build session**. The "Policy wording / summary" column below is taken from the project brief's description of those documents, not from the signed PDFs. Before production use, HR must compare each row against the signed documents and record any difference. Nothing in this table should be treated as officially approved company policy until that check is done.

**How confirmation works in the portal:** every configurable rule is a parameter of a versioned `PolicyVersion`. Parameters marked *needs HR* show **"Awaiting HR confirmation"** under *Policy Settings* until an administrator records who confirmed the interpretation and the reference (e.g. "HR Head email, 12 Nov 2026"). That record is stored on the version (`hr_confirmations`) and in the audit log. Changing a value creates a new version; earlier attendance/leave records keep a link to the version they were calculated with.

Legend — **Authority to confirm:** HR = HR Head; HR+Fin = HR Head and Finance Controller; Mgmt = Management.
